How bidding works

Every bid counts.

Buyers can submit an offer at any positive amount. The seller must approve the first offer before bidding starts. During the auction, each bid must be higher than the current highest bid; there is no fixed increment. The auction starts with 24 hours and each new bid adds 30 minutes.
